## Thanks

Trimcraft stands on the shoulders of these open source projects:

- [Tauri](https://tauri.app): the desktop app framework and its plugins
- [SDL](https://libsdl.org): joystick and HID input
- [Svelte](https://svelte.dev) and [Vite](https://vite.dev): the user interface
- [vJoy](https://github.com/BrunnerInnovation/vJoy): virtual joysticks
- [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org) and its crates, among them serde, windows-rs, keyring,
  rodio, ureq and thiserror
- [cargo-about](https://github.com/EmbarkStudios/cargo-about): the licence list

The licences of all third-party software in Trimcraft are listed in
[THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES).
